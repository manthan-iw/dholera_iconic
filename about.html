Design Philosophy

Architecture That Commands Attention

Every detail of ICONIC has been considered — from the ground plan to the roofline.

G+5 Structure

Six floors of premium commercial space, each with its own character — ground retail, showroom floors, and office levels above.

Full Glass Frontage

Floor-to-ceiling glazing on all commercial floors maximises natural light, reduces energy use, and creates a striking street presence.

Double-Height Lobbies

Grand entrance lobbies set the tone from the moment you walk in — conveying prestige, scale, and quality to every visitor.

24/7 Security

CCTV surveillance across all floors and parking areas, with trained on-site security staff around the clock.

Ample Parking

Dedicated basement and surface parking for tenants, customers, and visitors — a practical requirement that is well-integrated.

Green Building Principles

Energy-efficient glazing, LED lighting throughout, rainwater harvesting, and EV-ready parking bays integrated from the design stage.
