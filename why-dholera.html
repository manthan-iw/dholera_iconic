The Big Picture

A City Built from a Blank Canvas

Dholera Special Investment Region (DSIR) is a 920 sq km planned city on the Delhi–Mumbai Industrial Corridor (DMIC) — one of India's most strategically significant infrastructure projects. Unlike retrofitted cities, Dholera was planned from scratch: wide boulevards, underground utilities, smart traffic management, and industrial zones designed for 21st-century business.

With Tata Semiconductor's ₹91,000 crore fab plant already under construction, and global players like TSMC and Micron in active negotiations, Dholera is fast becoming India's semiconductor and tech manufacturing capital.
